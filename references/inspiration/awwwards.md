# Awwwards

> **URL:** https://www.awwwards.com · **Type:** Website awards (SOTD/SOTM/SOTY), collections, academy · **Captured:** 2026-11-11

## What it is
The canonical award for creative web: **Honor Mentions, Nominees, Sites of the Day/Month/Year, Honors, Most Awarded Profiles, Annual Awards** with a yearly jury. Trending collections: Portfolio Websites, Free fonts, Animated websites, Scrolling, One page design, UI design, E-commerce layouts. Each site page lists scores (design/usability/creativity/content) and often **tech stack tags** (GSAP, three.js, WebGL, Lenis, Webflow…). Also Awwwards Academy courses and Elements (component inspiration).
