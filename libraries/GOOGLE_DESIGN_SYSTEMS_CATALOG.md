## 2. How the 10 Google Systems Complement Local, External & `SaaS/` (`77f5dc6`) Systems

Our WDA design-system bench now spans four complementary layers with zero architectural blind spots:

1. **Spatial Cartography & Mobile Bottom-Sheet Pattern (`09-geo-terra`)**:
   - Local (`Switchyard`, `Civicgate`) and `SaaS/` admin dashboards excel at rectangular shells, whereas **Geo Terra** provides Google Maps' battle-tested `100vw × 100vh` floating omnibox, layer-chip bar, and single-DOM **Desktop left parcel card → Mobile snap bottom sheet** continuum for `#01`, `#02`, `#15`, `#35`, and `#37`.
2. **Ultra-Dense Enterprise & Financial Tabular Rigor (`02-pantheon-cloud-matter` + `03-acx-ads`)**:
   - Complements `Midday` and `Twenty CRM` with `28px–36px` multi-million-row resource tables (`Pantheon`) and frozen-total period-over-period variance scorecards (`ACX`) for `#02`, `#04`, `#07`, `#09`, `#11`–`#14`, and `#24`.
3. **Dark Cyber SIEM, NLE Media & Split-Screen Code Sandboxes (`06-secops-uno`, `07-youtube-yt-spec`, `10-devsite-codelabs`)**:
   - **SecOps UNO** adds native 4-tier severity telemetry and JSON/diff viewers alongside `Vercel Geist` (`#01, #05, #08, #26, #36`); **YouTube `yt-spec`** unlocks `#34` (`10/10` 4-pane AI Video NLE); **DevSite** solves `#27` (zero-purple Python IDE) and `#30` (step-by-step robot codelab).
4. **Grounded Citations & Global Multi-Script / RTL Typography (`08-search-sds`, `01-gm3-expressive`, `05-glue-marketing`)**:
   - **Search SDS** provides canonical cited RAG containers (`#20, #25`), while **GM3** and **Glue** supply verified Arabic RTL (`#20, #28`), Spanish (`#22, #35`), German (`#23`), and African multi-script (`#36`) typographic scales.
