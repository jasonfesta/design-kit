# 1.4 Distinguishable Visual and Audio Design

Criteria covered: 1.4.1 through 1.4.13. Use this reference for color, contrast, typography, audio control, text resize, reflow, non-text contrast, text spacing, images of text, background audio, and hover/focus content.

## 1.4.1 Use of Color

Color may reinforce meaning, but it cannot be the only signal. Pair color with text, icons, patterns, borders, shape plus text, programmatic state, or explicit labels.

Check links, errors, selected states, chart series, calendar status, required fields, warnings, success messages, and disabled states.

## 1.4.2 Audio Control

If audio plays automatically for more than 3 seconds, users need a way to pause, stop, mute, or control that audio independently from system volume. The control must be easy to find and operate immediately.

Avoid autoplay. Never let decorative audio interfere with screen reader output or user concentration.

## Contrast Requirements

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum (AA) | Text and images of text: 4.5:1; large text: 3:1. |
| 1.4.6 Contrast Enhanced (AAA) | Text and images of text: 7:1; large text: 4.5:1. |
| 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA) | Meaningful graphics and UI component boundaries/states: 3:1 against adjacent colors. |

Exceptions exist for inactive components, incidental text, logotypes, and essential presentations. Do not overuse exceptions to excuse low-contrast design tokens.

Test all states: default, hover, focus, active, selected, pressed, invalid, disabled-adjacent context, placeholder, visited, current, and high-contrast themes.

## 1.4.4 Resize Text

Text must resize to 200% without loss of content or functionality, except captions and images of text. Use flexible units, allow containers to grow, and avoid fixed heights that clip text.

Test browser zoom, text-only zoom where available, responsive breakpoints, dialogs, navigation, sticky headers, tables, cards, and form controls.

## 1.4.5 and 1.4.9 Images of Text

Use real text instead of images of text. Real text can scale, reflow, translate, recolor, and be read by assistive technology.

At AA, images of text are allowed when customizable or essential. At AAA, they are allowed only for pure decoration or essential presentation. Logos are generally treated as essential.

Check hero banners, promotions, screenshots, charts, infographics, buttons, thumbnails, and social embeds.

## 1.4.7 Low or No Background Audio

For prerecorded speech audio at AAA, background sounds must be absent, removable, or at least 20 dB lower than the foreground speech except for brief incidental sounds.

This applies to podcasts, voiceovers, tutorials, product demos, training, and announcements. Do not make comprehension depend on separating speech from music or ambient sound.

## 1.4.8 Visual Presentation

At AAA, blocks of text need a mechanism allowing users to achieve:

- selectable foreground and background colors;
- width no more than 80 characters or glyphs;
- text not fully justified;
- line spacing at least 1.5 within paragraphs;
- paragraph spacing at least 1.5 times line spacing;
- text resizing to 200% without horizontal scrolling in a full-screen window.

Even when not targeting AAA, these are strong readability defaults for content-heavy pages.

## 1.4.10 Reflow

Content must reflow without loss of information or functionality at 320 CSS px width and at 256 CSS px height, except content that requires two-dimensional layout for usage or meaning.

Valid two-dimensional exceptions can include data tables, maps, diagrams, images, video, games, and interfaces where both dimensions are necessary. Provide accessible navigation, summaries, or alternate views where possible.

Avoid horizontal page scrolling caused by fixed widths, long unbroken strings, sticky sidebars, oversized modals, fixed tables, or off-canvas navigation.

## 1.4.12 Text Spacing

Users must be able to override text spacing without content or functionality loss:

- line height to at least 1.5 times font size;
- paragraph spacing to at least 2 times font size;
- letter spacing to at least 0.12 times font size;
- word spacing to at least 0.16 times font size.

Do not clip, overlap, hide, or disable controls when these values are applied.

## 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus

When hover or focus reveals additional content that then disappears, it must be:

- dismissible without moving pointer or focus, unless it does not obscure other content;
- hoverable when triggered by pointer hover;
- persistent until hover/focus is removed, user dismisses it, or it is no longer valid.

This applies to custom tooltips, nonmodal popovers, flyouts, submenus, and hover cards. Native browser title tooltips are controlled by the user agent and are not reliable for essential information.

## Design Token Requirements

Define tokens for:

- text/background contrast at AA and optional AAA;
- non-text component boundaries and states;
- focus indicators and focus contrast;
- error, warning, success, selected, and disabled states with non-color cues;
- typography spacing that tolerates user overrides;
- responsive containers that reflow at narrow widths.

## Acceptance Tests

- Measure text and non-text contrast in all states and themes.
- Verify meaning is preserved without color.
- Resize text to 200% and complete tasks.
- Test 320 CSS px width or 400% zoom.
- Apply text-spacing overrides.
- Review images of text and replace nonessential cases.
- Test hover/focus content with keyboard, pointer, and dismissal.
- Confirm audio does not start unexpectedly or can be controlled.

## Common Failures

- Placeholder text or disabled-looking active controls with low contrast.
- Error state shown only with red border.
- Focus indicator hidden by low contrast or reset styles.
- Fixed-height cards clipping enlarged text.
- Tooltip disappears when the pointer moves toward it.
- Modal or table wider than the viewport at 320 px.
- Text embedded in promotional images.
- Background audio competing with speech.
