# 2.3 Seizures, Motion, and Vestibular Safety

Criteria covered: 2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold (A), 2.3.2 Three Flashes (AAA), and 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions (AAA). Use this reference for animation, video, ads, transitions, loaders, parallax, scroll effects, and interaction-triggered motion.

## Core Requirement

Visual effects must not create seizure risk or physical discomfort. Motion can trigger vestibular disorders, migraines, nausea, disorientation, anxiety, and loss of focus. Treat motion and flashing as user-impact risks, not decoration defaults.

## 2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold

At Level A, content must not flash more than three times in one second unless the flash is below the defined general and red flash thresholds.

High-risk content:

- video clips and GIFs;
- loading animations;
- error flashes;
- ads and promotional banners;
- game-like effects;
- emergency or warning animations;
- rapid color inversions;
- strobe-like transitions.

Do not assume small or brief content is safe. Test before publishing if flashing approaches the threshold.

## 2.3.2 Three Flashes

At AAA, the rule is stricter: no content flashes more than three times in any one-second period. Use this as the internal default when possible, because it is easier to govern than threshold calculations.

## 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions

At AAA, motion animation triggered by interaction must be disableable unless essential to the functionality or information.

Interaction-triggered motion includes:

- parallax and scroll-linked movement;
- page transitions;
- zooming or panning effects;
- animated sorting/filtering;
- drag animations;
- expanding cards with large movement;
- swipes that slide whole panels;
- progress animations after a user action.

Respect user reduced-motion preferences and provide product-level controls where motion is a significant part of the experience.

## Safer Motion Rules

Prefer:

- opacity changes over large spatial movement;
- short, subtle transitions over long animations;
- no auto-start motion for decorative effects;
- reduced-motion variants that remove movement rather than merely speeding it up;
- user-triggered playback for animated media;
- static fallback states for loaders and illustrations;
- clear start/stop controls for rich animations.

Avoid:

- full-screen zoom, spin, flip, or parallax;
- motion tied directly to scroll position;
- looping animations near reading content;
- rapid flashing color changes;
- red flashes;
- shaking UI to indicate errors;
- animations that move the user's point of attention away from the task.

## Reduced-Motion Behavior

When reduced motion is requested:

- remove nonessential transforms, parallax, smooth scrolling, and animated route transitions;
- keep state changes understandable through instant changes, fades, text, or static cues;
- preserve functionality and feedback;
- do not hide content or controls simply because animation is disabled;
- ensure loaders and progress states remain perceivable without motion.

## Media and Ad Governance

Content from marketing, ads, social embeds, and third parties can still violate seizure and motion criteria. Review animated content before publication. If third-party content cannot be controlled, it must not interfere with the rest of the page and may need to be excluded from conformance scope with appropriate partial conformance handling.

## Acceptance Tests

- Inventory flashing, blinking, animated, auto-playing, and interaction-triggered effects.
- Check whether any content flashes more than three times per second.
- Review video, GIF, and ad content before publishing.
- Enable reduced-motion settings and verify nonessential motion is suppressed.
- Trigger route changes, scrolling, sorting, filtering, opening, closing, and validation states.
- Confirm users can pause or stop moving content where 2.2.2 also applies.
- Verify motion reduction does not remove feedback or functionality.

## Common Failures

- Error state indicated by shaking the field with no reduced-motion alternative.
- Page transitions still slide/zoom under reduced motion.
- Animated ads flash or loop beside primary content.
- Loader flashes rapidly while content loads.
- Carousel, video, or GIF starts automatically and cannot be stopped.
- Smooth scrolling causes large forced movement after navigation.
- Reduced-motion mode only shortens animation duration instead of removing vestibular motion.
