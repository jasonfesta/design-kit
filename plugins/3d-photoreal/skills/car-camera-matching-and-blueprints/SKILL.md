# Automotive Camera Matching, Wheel Conics & Blueprint Fusion (`automotive-threejs`)

If lens focal length (`FOV`), camera crouch height, wheel-rim ellipse foreshortening, or wheelbase-to-track proportions disagree with the reference plate by even `3%`, even flawless clearcoat shaders will read as a distorted toy model.

## [LOCKED] First-Principles Axioms
<!-- BEGIN LOCKED AXIOMS -->
1. **Canonical Right-Handed Metric Vehicle Datum (`1 unit = 1.000 m`):** Lock every vehicle and camera to a single coordinate contract before modeling: `y = 0.000` is the loaded tire contact-patch ground plane (`+Y` up), `x = 0.000` is the longitudinal bilateral symmetry plane (`+X` vehicle left/port, `-X` vehicle right/starboard), and `z = 0.000` is the wheelbase midpoint (`+Z` forward nose, front axle at `+0.5 * L_wb`, rear axle at `-0.5 * L_wb`, `det([+X, +Y, +Z]) === +1`).
2. **Exact Native Aspect Probe & Telephoto-First FOV Law:** Always inspect `<ref.jpg>` (and any secondary `VIEW 2..N` or blueprints) via `read_file` and run `python3 ~/design-kit/skills/car-camera-matching-and-blueprints/scripts/probe_car_reference.py <ref.jpg> [--blueprint <path>]`. Lock canvas dimensions strictly to `recommended_canvas_size` (`W × H`), and never default to `45°–55°` WebGL FOV on studio/concours plates (`85–200 mm` telephoto = `10°–23°` vertical FOV).
3. **Wheel-Rim Conic & Bilateral Horizon VP Law:** Unsteered rear wheel rims (`δ_r = 0°`, camber `γ_r`) are exact metric circles of radius `R_rim` whose projected ellipse aspect ratio `b_r / a_r ≈ |n_hub · v_ray|` locks axle viewing angle, while steered front wheel ellipses (`b_f / a_f`) solve steering lock `δ_f`. Intersecting `>= 4` bilateral symmetry chords (`headlamp corners`, `A-pillar bases`, `mirror stalks`, `taillamp corners`) gives transverse VP `(u_lat, v_lat)` and longitudinal sill/beltline rails give `(u_long, v_long)`, fixing horizon line, Dutch roll `φ = atan2(v_lat - v_long, u_lat - u_long)`, and focal scale `f_u = sqrt((uPrincipal - u_min) * (u_max - uPrincipal))`.
4. **`OrbitControls` Target Order & Cropped Principal-Point Invariant:** `new OrbitControls(camera, domElement)` calls `controls.update()` toward `(0, 0, 0)`, corrupting crouched camera pitch and Dutch roll. Always set `controls.target` and invoke `controls.update()` **before** applying `camera.position`, `camera.up` (for roll), `camera.lookAt(...)`, and `camera.setViewOffset(W, H, (0.5 - uPrincipal) * W, (0.5 - vPrincipal) * H, W, H)`.
5. **Cross-View Blueprint Scale & Axle Co-Registration:** Never trace raw multi-view blueprint pixels without stripping dimension callout margins and co-registering all four orthographic views (`Side`, `Front`, `Rear`, `Top`) to identical metric extrema (`L_side === L_top`, `H_side === H_front === H_rear`, `W_front === W_rear === W_top`, front/rear axle centers locked at `z = ±0.5 * L_wb`).

## [REGIME PLAYBOOK] Conditional Rules
<!-- BEGIN LEARNED LAWS -->
- **WHEN `perspective_focal_regime == "telephoto_studio_85_200mm"` (Rear/Front wheel height ratio `>= 0.78`):** Place the camera `7.5–18.0 m` away at crouched hub-to-beltline elevation (`y_cam ≈ 0.42–1.05 m`) with `fov = 10°–22°`. Never move a wide camera closer to fill the frame—doing so balloons the front bumper and shrinks the rear quarter panel.
- **WHEN `perspective_focal_regime == "wide_dynamic_rig_20_32mm"` (Rear/Front wheel height ratio `< 0.68` or `|dutch_roll_hint_deg| >= 2.5°`):** Place the tracking-boom camera `2.1–4.4 m` from the nose/quarter at `fov = 38°–62°`, apply true camera roll via `camera.up.set(Math.sin(roll), Math.cos(roll), 0)` before `lookAt`, and keep horizontal FOV `<= 85°` rectilinear (or multi-frustum stereographic above `95°`).
- **WHEN editorial framing places the car off-center (`|uPrincipal - 0.5| > 0.06` or generous upper sky/cove negative space):** Solve optical center `(uPrincipal, vPrincipal)` from `v_horizon` and wheel-ellipse major-axis perpendiculars and apply `camera.setViewOffset(...)` rather than pitching the camera away from the car—pitching introduces vertical keystoning on shutlines and B-pillars.
- **WHEN technical drawings or 4-view blueprints are provided alongside photographs:** Record a confidence-tagged dimensional fact sheet (`L`, `W_body`, `W_mirrors`, `H`, `L_wb`, `T_f`, `T_r`, `R_tire`, `R_rim`, `ground_clearance`) locking OEM/blueprint dimensions as ground truth while solving camera pose `(position, target, fov, principalPoint, rollDeg)` and dynamic suspension compression/steering angle `δ_f` from `<ref.jpg>`.
- **WHEN reconstructing solely from photographs (`VIEW 1..N`) without blueprints:** Anchor scale to known automotive invariants (standard rim diameters `15"–21"` -> `R_rim`, license-plate standard `520×110 mm` EU or `305×152 mm` US, or published OEM wheelbase `L_wb`), triangulate 3D landmarks across `VIEW 1..N`, and register every secondary photo in `window.__CAR_CAMERAS__` (`{ name, refPhoto, position, target, fov, principalPoint, rollDeg, steerDeg, exposureBias }`).
- **WHEN matching indoor motor-show / concours stanchion-rope plates (`press barrier 3.5–5.8 m from vehicle center`):** Do not blindly force `85–200 mm` (`10°–18°`) telephoto distances onto barrier-rope exhibition shots—press photographers standing at the stanchion rope use `28–55 mm` lenses (`fov = 20°–38°`) with off-center crop/aim (`principalPoint` via `camera.setViewOffset`). Solve a full **9-DOF PnP fit** (`[cx, cy, cz, tx, ty, tz, fov, uPrincipal, vPrincipal, rollDeg]`) against `>= 8` metric 3D vehicle landmarks (`wheel hubs`, `windshield corners`, `nose/tail badges`, `wing/spoiler tips`, `floor graphic markers`) independently for `VIEW 1` and each `VIEW 2..N` at their exact native aspect ratios (`W / H`).
- **WHEN matching wheel contact patches on cambered or sloped roads (`roadHeight(x, z)`):** Intersect view rays with `roadHeight(x, z)` via 32-step monotonic bisection, fit the chassis plane to all four contact patches `(±T_f/2, +L_wb/2)` and `(±T_r/2, -L_wb/2)`, and verify `< 0.5 px` silhouette lock on tire-to-ground tangency via 2-render empirical secants (`du_px / d_param`).
