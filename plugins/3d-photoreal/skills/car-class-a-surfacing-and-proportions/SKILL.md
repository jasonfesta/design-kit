# Automotive Class-A Surfacing, Stance & 3D Cabin Craft (`automotive-threejs`)

A car reads as coachbuilt industrial design—never a bulbous soap bar or extruded box—only when its **hardpoint wheel stance, plan-view fuselage sweep, monotone-spline feature rails, positive panel crown, flanged wheelhouses, `3.5 mm` shutlines, and 3D cabin interior** obey manufacturing geometry.

## [LOCKED] First-Principles Axioms
<!-- BEGIN LOCKED AXIOMS -->
1. **Hardpoint Chassis First (`Wheel-Diameter Proportion Lock`):** Construct and lock the 4-wheel rolling chassis datum (`wheelbase L_wb`, `front/rear track T_f, T_r`, `tire radius R_tire`, `rim radius R_rim`, `dash-to-axle offset`, `cowl/roof/beltline heights`) **before** skinning body panels. Constrain tires to `y = 0.000` with `4–8 mm` smooth vertical contact-patch deflection and `-0.8°..-2.4°` camber so `1,400+ kg` mass reads immediately.
2. **Overshoot-Free Monotone Hermite Rails & Mandatory Panel Crown (`car_surface_kernel.js`):** **NEVER** loft vehicle stations with unclamped Catmull-Rom splines (which bulge roofs and bow flat rocker sills) or intersect unblended primitive spheres/boxes. Interpolate longitudinal feature rails (`undertray`, `rocker`, `shoulder/swage`, `beltline`, `greenhouse rail`, `centerline`) via **Fritsch–Carlson monotone piecewise cubic Hermite splines**, and give every nominally flat hood, roof, and door skin **`8–22 mm` positive barrel crown** so studio light ribbons never oil-can.
3. **Flanged Wheel Arches & Zero Cross-Car Daylight Leaks:** Cut wheel-arch openings radially around `(±T/2, y_arch, ±0.5 * L_wb)` with **`14–22 mm` stamped inward sheet-metal lip return flanges** backed by continuous matte-black inner wheelhouse tubs and a full underbody floorpan (`albedo ~ 0.015, roughness: 0.92`)—never leave paper-thin single-sided fender cutouts or daylight tunnels between wheels.
4. **Dihedral Crease Hierarchy, `3.5 mm` Rolled Shutlines & Rest-Pose UV Lock:** Blend vertex normals by dihedral angle (`smooth <= 3.5°` across fender crowns, crisp split `>= 14.5°` along character creases), recess stamped panel partings (`hood`, `doors`, `bumpers`, `decklid`) as `3.0–4.5 mm` dark channels with `1.5–2.5 mm` rolled edge lips (twin opposing edge glints), and bake assembled world-meter attributes (`aRestPos`, `aRestNormal`) **prior** to hinge detachment so metallic flakes, carbon weave, and livery stripes never swim when doors or hoods open.
5. **Complete 3D Cabin Interior & Caliper-vs-Hub Kinematic Split:** **NEVER** black out windows (`< 8 sRGB`) to hide an empty shell. Model a full 3D cockpit (sculpted dash/binnacle, steering wheel & column, bolstered seats, center console, door cards, A/B/C-pillar inner trim, headliner, central mirror), and mount brake calipers on the non-spinning steering knuckle (`knuckleGroup`) while spinning only `hubGroup` (wheel rim + rotor + tire) under Ackermann geometry (`cot δ_out - cot δ_in = T_f / L_wb`).

## [REGIME PLAYBOOK] Conditional Rules
<!-- BEGIN LEARNED LAWS -->
- **WHEN lofting three-box sedans, fastback GTs, or mid-engine wedges:** Sculpt true **plan-view fuselage sweep** (`Top` view)—tapering nose and tail `6–14%` narrower than peak fender flares over the axles, pinching door flanks subtle Coke-bottle inward between wheels, and slanting the greenhouse inward (`14°–26°` tumblehome, roof rail `0.70–0.82 × maxBodyWidth`) rather than extruding a 2D side silhouette straight across `X`.
- **WHEN modeling aero splitters, rear wings, louvers, or diffuser strakes:** Space chordwise profile vertices via **cosine clustering** (`u_k = 0.5 * (1 - cos(pi * k / M))`) so tight `2–4 mm` leading-edge radii hold smooth specular highlights while trailing Gurney flaps close crisply without excessive triangles.
- **WHEN sculpting multi-spoke forged/cast alloy wheels, lug pockets, and ventilated rotors:** Author one radial spoke sector with filleted spoke-to-barrel transitions, recessed lug-bolt pockets, valve stem, and center cap, then replicate cleanly across `N_spokes` (`mergeGeometries` or `InstancedMesh` with `instanceMatrix` world derivatives), placing cross-drilled or slotted iron/carbon-ceramic brake rotors + multi-piston calipers behind the spokes at realistic rotor-to-barrel radial clearance (`~18–35 mm`).
- **WHEN articulating doors, gullwings, clamshell hoods, active aero, or pop-up headlamps:** Expose `window.__CAR_MECHANISMS__.set(name, t)` (`t in [0, 1]`) driven by fifth-order minimum-jerk trajectories (`10*t^3 - 15*t^4 + 6*t^5`), and sweep `t in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0}` to verify zero fender-to-door hinge interpenetration and flush `0.0 mm` shutline alignment at `t = 0`.
- **WHEN skinning curved front/rear bumpers, fastback greenhouses, and Panoramic / Lexan roofs (`car_surface_kernel.js`):**
  1. Fade pontoon-fender lateral relief (`pontoonFade = sX^2`) smoothly into superelliptical bumper corners so `Front`/`Rear` elevations never form flat horizontal shelf steps above curved intakes.
  2. Weld the side greenhouse (`C-pillar sail`, `cantrail arch`, `A-pillar post`, and `lower sill skirt`) as one continuous `C^1` coachbuilt mesh (`>= 44` longitudinal stations along `u = z`) using piecewise-matched `BOT_ROWS + TOP_ROWS` row parameterization at `z = dloZRear` and `z = dloZFront` (`0.0 mm` T-junction-free vertex weld so zero vertical normal crease forms behind the quarter window), taut linear tumblehome (`9 mm` barrel bow + `hFrac^5` cantrail fillet—never a ballooning quadratic Bezier bulge), a forward-raked painted front windshield header bridge + `14 mm` upper weatherstrip seal across steep windshields, convex C-pillar crowning (`cPillarCrown > 0`) onto wide rear haunches while dropping `yCenterPts` inside the cabin, and body-colored outer cantrails plus a transverse rear header bridge around panoramic sunroofs.
  3. Keep `zCabinRear` (`cockpitDrop`) strictly ahead of tapered fastback C-pillars (`unless covered by a slotted mid-engine Lexan window`) and upholster the full rear cabin ramp in dark trim so cross-cabin sightlines never expose bright interior body-color sheet metal.
- **WHEN official OpenUSD / CAD sub-assemblies exist vs. pure photo/blueprint builds:** Compose `.usd`/CAD stages offline in Node+WASM (`Int16` `0.25 mm` precision, normal-aware exterior clustering, Z-up to Y-up conversion) with zero runtime `Worker` fallbacks on opaque-origin x20 sandboxes; otherwise generate watertight manifold procedural meshes via `scripts/car_surface_kernel.js` within `< 250` draw calls.
