# Genre Cartridge: Tactical First-Person Shooter (`Task Force: Zero Hour` Dossier)

Enforce these **15 Non-Negotiable Genre Laws** and **10-File Module Contracts** whenever building a First-Person Shooter (`FPS`) or Tactical Action game in Three.js.

---

## 1. The 15 Non-Negotiable FPS Laws

1. **2-Node `YXZ` Gimbal-Safe Camera + Unparented `worldCamProxy`**: Separate yaw (`playerRoot` at feet `(x, y, z)`) from pitch/roll (`cameraPitchPivot` with `rotation.order = 'YXZ'` clamped to `+-82 deg`), and synchronize an unparented `worldCamProxy` every frame (`worldCamProxy.position.copy(worldEyePos)`, `worldCamProxy.quaternion.copy(worldEyeQuat)`) so no subsystem ever confuses local `(0, 0, 0)` with world eye position.
2. **Depth-Cleared Dual-FOV Viewmodel Overlay (`fovVm = 46 deg -> 28 deg` vs. `fovMain = 78 deg -> 52 deg`)**: Render the first-person weapon and arms in a separate `viewmodelScene` after `renderer.clearDepth()` so the barrel never clips into walls and maintains telephoto Gunsmith proportions.
3. **World SSAO Before `clearDepth()`**: Execute 12-tap Golden-Angle Spiral Screen-Space Ambient Occlusion + 7-tap depth-bilateral blur on `sceneRT.depthTexture` *before* clearing depth for the viewmodel pass, and merge all static prop ground shadow skirts (`y = 0.012 m`) into a 1-draw-call `WorldContactAO` mesh.
4. **Exact Tangent-FOV Laser & Crosshair Convergence (`0.000 px` Error)**: Project world raycast hit distance `wDist` into viewmodel space via `dVm = clamp(wDist * (Math.tan(fovMain * 0.5) / Math.tan(fovVm * 0.5)), 0.42, 135.0)` and terminate the PEQ-15 laser beam and surface dot at `(0.0, 0.0, -dVm)` along `viewmodelCamera`'s optical axis *after* `weaponPivot.updateMatrixWorld(true)`.
5. **Collimated Holographic Eyebox Gating (`adsAmount >= 0.78`)**: Hide reflex/EOTech reticles when the rifle is canted in hip-fire (`adsAmount < 0.78`) and project `(0.0, 0.0, curModel.adsEyeZ)` into optic-local coordinates during ADS so the reticle tracks true bore parallax inside the optic window.
6. **Dual-Render Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Sniper Scope (`512x512` `scopeRT`)**: For high-power scopes (`8.0x`), render the world a second time at narrow FOV (`9.5 deg`) into a dedicated `WebGLRenderTarget` mapped onto the scope ocular lens with etched MIL-dot stadia and eye-relief vignette.
7. **40–48 Radial Segments & Zero Cast-Iron Bump on CNC Receivers**: Foreground weapons sit `15–40 cm` from the camera; use `40–48` radial segments on barrels/suppressors/scopes, slotted Picatinny/M-LOK rails, and directional brushed anodized roughness (`0.28..0.36`, `metalness: 0.78..0.86`, **zero** coarse Perlin/rock bump noise).
8. **46-Ring Nyquist-Safe BDU Sleeves + Wrist Bridge**: Construct first-person forearms with `>= 44` longitudinal rings × `28` radial segments and low-frequency (`2–4` cycle) elbow compression folds, bridging the cuff-to-hand seam with a Velcro strap, tactical watch (`Casio G-Shock`), and flared `Mechanix` glove gauntlet (`0` wrist gaps).
9. **Suppressed Gas Jet vs. Unsuppressed Multi-Port Brake Chiaroscuro**: Suppressed weapons emit only a narrow forward gas jet (`opacity <= 0.38`, `0` star fireballs); unsuppressed rifles emit multi-port side/top fireballs + pre-allocated dynamic `PointLight` bounce (`intensity = 0` at idle, `1.65..3.8` on fire, `0` shader recompiles).
10. **120 Hz Sub-Stepped Mobility Suite (`Walk / Sprint / Tac-Sprint / Slide / Mantle / Q-E Lean`)**: Sub-step capsule physics at `1/120 s` supporting Walk (`4.8 m/s`), Sprint (`7.6 m/s`), double-Shift Tactical Sprint (`9.2 m/s`), `C` Tactical Slide (`10.8 m/s` with `-7.5 deg` camera roll), contextual obstacle Mantle (`0.55 m <= topY - feetY <= 1.55 m`), and collision-clamped `Q/E` corner lean (`+-10 deg`, `+-0.34 m`).
11. **Height-Aware Collider Signature & Per-Zone 3D Capsule Hitboxes**: Enforce `world.resolveHorizontal(x, yFeet, z, radius, height)` (`yFeet` is argument #2!). Raycast bullets against bone-attached 3D capsules (`intersectRayCapsule`) with center-mass priority (`head: 2.5x`, `torso: 1.0x`, `limbs: 0.65x`), and intersect enemy shots against the player's 3D vertical capsule (`foot` to `head`, `r = 0.39 m`).
12. **3-Phase Squad AI (`96x96` Binary-Heap `A*` + 2-Pass Capsule Separation + Post-Collision Gait)**: Combine `1.0 m` octile `A*` pathfinding (`rClear = 0.68 m`) + LOS string-pulling + 5-whisker steering (`0, +-28, +-58 deg`), 2-pass symmetric capsule separation (`minSep = 1.08 m..1.24 m`), and `stridePhase` driven strictly by post-collision displacement (`distMoved / fullCycleMeters`) with `cosLeg > 0` swing knee lift, 3-phase ankle roll, and decoupled `hipsYawCurrent`.
13. **Topological Wet Asphalt (`smoothstep(0.46, 0.68)`) & `640x360` Lengyel Planar Reflections**: Partition wet tarmac into `35%` discrete standing puddles (`roughness = 0.035`, `640x360` `HalfFloatType` Lengyel oblique-clipped planar reflection + raindrop ripples) and `65%` crushed-basalt aggregate (`roughness = 0.72..0.88`, `normalScale = 0.95`), keeping painted lane stripes matte (`roughness >= 0.78`).
14. **Synchronized `0.0 deg` Azimuth Compass & `12-o'clock` Rotating Radar**: Convert Three.js yaw to compass azimuth via `((-yaw * 180 / Math.PI) % 360 + 360) % 360`, lock the radar player cone to `-Math.PI / 2` (`12 o'clock`), and rotate the radar map + `N` badge by `-azimuth`.
15. **Orthogonal `90 deg` Spec-Ops Armory (`>= 65%` Open 3D Vista) & Frameless HOD (`< 8%` Occlusion)**: Ban `clip-path: polygon(...)` chamfers, `skewX` bars, and `//` prefixes; dock Gunsmith weapon cards (`fill-rule="evenodd"` negative-space CAD SVGs) on a `382 px` left rail with top navigation tabs, and float borderless combat telemetry at screen edges.
