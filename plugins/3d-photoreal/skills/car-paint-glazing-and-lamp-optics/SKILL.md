# Automotive Paint, Laminated Glazing & Lamp Optics (`automotive-threejs`)

Automotive photorealism lives or dies on **multi-layer clearcoat reflection transport, see-through ceramic-frit glazing, 3-depth lamp optics, and non-blowing-out satin-black elastomers**.

## [LOCKED] First-Principles Axioms
<!-- BEGIN LOCKED AXIOMS -->
1. **3-Layer OEM Paint Stack (`Basecoat Flop + fwidth Flakes + IOR 1.50 Clearcoat`):** Never shade glossy car bodies with single-lobe `MeshStandardMaterial`. Pair a linear pigment basecoat (with `(N·V)^p` grazing metallic/mica flop and white diffuse peaks `<= 0.75` linear under `pbrNeutral`) with rest-pose world-meter (`aRestPos`) micro-flakes attenuated via `fwidth` into LEADR roughness variance (`0%` far-field sparkle boil) beneath a refractive polyurethane clearcoat (`clearcoat: 1.0, ior: 1.50, clearcoatRoughness: 0.015–0.042`) carrying gentle `3.5–6.0 mm` micro-orange-peel (`normal slope <= 0.007`).
2. **Exact Dielectric Fresnel Glazing & Silk-Screened Ceramic Frit Border:** Evaluate exact unpolarized $s/p$ Fresnel (`globesFresnel`, critical angle `41.8°`) + soda-lime iron path absorption `exp(-vec3(0.30, 0.10, 0.12) * path)` with clear head-on transmission (`opacity <= 0.06` on untinted windshields so the 3D cabin reads cleanly). Frame every flush-bonded windshield, backlight, and quarter pane with a **`18–35 mm` matte-black ceramic frit perimeter + `4–6`-row halftone dot-matrix transition** hiding adhesive pinchwelds.
3. **3-Depth Optical Lamp Assemblies (`Zero Flat Emissive Stickers`):** Build every headlamp, foglamp, and taillamp with three real 3D optical layers: (a) a recessed dark housing (`0x08090c`) + aluminized parabolic bowls or solid `IOR 1.585` projector/light-pipe optics (zero crevice AO mud on clear optics), (b) HDR emitter cores retaining non-zero minor channels (`G >= 0.035*R, B >= 0.012*R` on ruby LEDs so `chroma_clip_pct <= 0.25%`), and (c) a separate flush curved outer polycarbonate cover lens catching continuous body reflections.
4. **Satin-Black Elastomer Anti-Blowout & Tangential Lathe Anisotropy:** Clamp vulcanized tires, rubber window seals, wiper blades, and raw-ABS splitters to `roughness: 0.68–0.86, specularIntensity: 0.16–0.24, clearcoat: 0.0` so default dielectric `F0 = 0.04` never bleaches tires pale grey under high-key studio scrims or noon sun. Orient circular lathe brushing on diamond-cut wheels and brake rotors **strictly tangentially (`(-y, x)`)** with `fwidth` roughness filtering.
5. **Shader Cache Hygiene & `InstancedMesh` World Derivatives:** Every `onBeforeCompile` material factory branching on JS closure options must define a deterministic `mat.customProgramCacheKey = () => ...`, avoid GLSL ES 3.00 reserved identifiers (`sample`, `patch`, `smooth`, `flat`, `filter`, `centroid`), and pre-multiply `#ifdef USE_INSTANCING\n  wp = instanceMatrix * wp;\n#endif` before `modelMatrix * wp`.

## [REGIME PLAYBOOK] Conditional Rules
<!-- BEGIN LEARNED LAWS -->
- **WHEN matching metallic, pearlescent, solid heritage lacquer, or satin/matte frozen finishes:** Use `createOEMCarPaintMaterial(...)` in `scripts/car_materials_and_optics.js`: set `flakeStrength: 0.0, orangePeelSlope: 0.004` for hand-rubbed classic single-stage coachwork; `flakeStrength: 0.35–0.65` with grazing hue/value flop for modern metallic/mica coats; and `clearcoatRoughness: 0.26–0.38` for factory satin/frozen finishes.
- **WHEN shading exposed `2×2` twill carbon fiber (splitters, roofs, mirrors, monocoques):** Alternate orthotropic fiber tangent orientation `90°` between warp and weft `2.5 mm` cells beneath a smooth `clearcoat: 1.0, clearcoatRoughness: 0.025` resin topcoat, clamping sub-pixel weave contrast via `1.0 / (1.0 + pow(fw / 0.0025, 2.0))` to prevent moiré.
- **WHEN detailing classic sealed-beam fluted headlamps vs. modern matrix-LED / OLED light blades:** For vintage cars (`pre-1992`), refract parabolic chrome reflector bowls through vertical cylindrical Fresnel lens flutes on the inner glass face; for modern cars, model dark-bezel projector condenser lenses (`n = 1.585`) framed by crisp acrylic DRL light-pipes (`roughness: 0.08` diffuse-scatter core).
- **WHEN placing honeycomb, mesh, or slatted front/rear intake grilles:** Always place a dark recessed 3D radiator/intercooler core (`6–14 cm` behind the grille face, subtle vertical fin texture) so zero bright studio cyclorama or sky background ever leaks through intake openings.
- **WHEN cutting recessed headlamp buckets, oval fender lamps, or NACA ducts into Class-A body skins:** **NEVER** depress coarse `(u, v)` body-grid vertices (`surfaceDepressionFn`), which creates jagged diagonal sawtooth tears in clearcoat Zebra stripes. Cut openings per-pixel via analytic SDF `apertures` (`'ellipsePair'`, `'rectPair'`, `'nacaPair'`) in `createOEMCarPaintMaterial` (`discard;` + subtle `0.065 rad` stamped lip bevel to avoid white edge halos), build headlamp buckets with `openEnded: true` matte-obsidian sidewalls plus a separate rear floor cap at `-0.080 m` (never a closed cylinder whose top lid buries the optics), terrace multi-point DRL pods individually on the sloped `unit` XY plane (`z_unit = -0.025..-0.036 m`) while aiming each pod's optical axis horizontally forward (`+Z_world`) so zero shroud or DRL corners graze the outer polycarbonate dome on `58°` sloped fenders, and shade clear covers with un-premultiplied dielectric Fresnel (`zero alpha*alpha double attenuation`).
- **WHEN shading solid non-metallic heritage enamels (`Rosso Corsa`, `Guards Red`, `Signal Yellow`) vs. metallic coats:** Set `metalness <= 0.02`, `flakeStrength: 0.0`, raise basecoat `roughness` to `0.32–0.38` beneath a razor-sharp `clearcoat: 1.0, clearcoatRoughness: 0.006` topcoat, and scale **both** `reflectedLight.directSpecular` and `clearcoatSpecularDirect` by `uDirectSpecScale` (`~0.025` for solid enamels, `~0.12–0.15` for metallics) after `#include <lights_fragment_end>` so shadow-casting `DirectionalLight`s never stamp circular Phong sunspots onto clearcoat while preserving 100% of `PMREM` area reflections.
- **WHEN wet-weather droplets or wiper sweeps appear on windshields and paint:** Populate `~98%` pinned multi-lobed sessile contact drops (`R(φ) = R0 * (1 + a * noise)`) plus `~2%` Bond-critical runners laying static `0.35×`-width wet tracks behind the drop, and gate concentric wiper-arc micro-glints tightly around `dot(tangent_wiper, H) ≈ 0` toward bright light sources.
