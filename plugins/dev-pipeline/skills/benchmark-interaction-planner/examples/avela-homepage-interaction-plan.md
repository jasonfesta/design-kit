## 5. The 3 Mandatory Checkpoints (Visual Gradient Scale, Interaction Completeness, and Benchmark Feel)

### Checkpoint 1 — Visual Checkpoint (Exact Desktop + Mobile Fidelity & Smooth Responsive Gradient Scale)
1. **1:1 Visual Lock to the Two Exclusive Authorities (`s_total >= 0.93+`)**:
   - **Desktop (`1440px` / `725 × 2170`)**: Matches Authority #1 ([`desktop-01-avela.png`](./homepage-study/concepts/01-avela/desktop-01-avela.png)) 1:1 at `t=0` across all 7 compositions—hero campaign composition (`y=0..404px`), freestanding unboxed bags on `#FBF6EE` cream, 55% left pale-blue Arc flat-lay, right-hand terracotta leather-scoring photo with offset lower-left stitch close-up, magazine-spread coastal carry + 2 offset community portraits, 3 hairline care disclosures beside macro leather/clasp detail, pale-blue inline newsletter bar, and 4-column oxblood footer.
   - **Mobile (`390 × 3210` & `320px`)**: Matches Authority #2 ([`mobile-01-avela.jpg`](./homepage-study/concepts/01-avela/mobile-01-avela.jpg)) 1:1 at `t=0`—compact `AVELA` + `Menu` / `Bag (0)` header, portrait campaign crop with 2-line `SOFT / FORM`, full-width lead `The Arc ($420)` above 2-up `The Fold ($360)` + `The Day ($480)`, full-width pale-blue flat-lay above copy, vertical making + stitch stack, portrait coastal story above 2-up `A weekend away →` + `A familiar favorite →`, full-width care rows + `Notes from the workshop →` photo strip, stacked full-width newsletter input + button, and compact oxblood footer.
2. **Smooth Responsive Gradient Scale (`320px` → `390px` → `430px` → `768px` → `1024px` → `1440px` → `1920px`)**:
   - **`320px` (Narrow Phone WCAG Reflow)**: `20px` side gutters; secondary 2-up rows stack cleanly if labels wrap; `0px` horizontal overflow (`scrollWidth <= clientWidth`).
   - **`390px`–`430px` (Standard / Large Phone)**: `24px` side gutters, `48–72px` chapter pauses, `16px` body copy, `32–40px` serif headings, 1-lead + 2-secondary product grid, and 2-up secondary community stories.
   - **`768px`–`1023px` (Tablet Continuum)**: Smooth transition from stacked mobile reading flow to proportional 2-/3-column grids without abrupt negative margin collisions; desktop negative offsets engage only at `>= 1024px`.
   - **`1024px`–`1920px+` (Desktop Continuum)**: Fluid side gutters `clamp(24px, 6vw, 96px)`, chapter pauses `clamp(96px, 11vw, 192px)`, and fluid typographic scale so the page breathes naturally across laptop and wide desktop monitors.

### Checkpoint 2 — Interaction Checkpoint (100% High-Craft Interactive Coverage on Desktop & Mobile)
- **Zero Dead Links or Unwired Controls**:
  - Every navigation item (`The bags`, `Our workshop`, `Care & repair`, mobile `Menu` dialog, `Bag (0)` drawer), product link (`Meet the Arc ↗`, `Explore the bags ↗`, `The Arc`, `The Fold`, `The Day`, `Discover the Arc ↗`), sibling color swatch (`Oxblood`, `Tan`, `Olive`, `Stone`), editorial story link (`Read the workshop story ↗`, `The everyday carry`, `A weekend away →`, `A familiar favorite →`, `Notes from the workshop →`), native `<details>/<summary>` row (`What fits`, `Leather care`, `Repairs`), `NewsletterForm` (`Join the atelier`), and footer link has a complete interactive state.
  - Clicking any bag or story link opens a rich, self-contained **Avela Dossier / Quick-Shop Drawer** (with live variant selection and `"Add to bag"` integration) or scrolls smoothly to its target chapter, while keeping `t=0` 100% clean.
- **Strict Separation of Link vs. Button Semantics**:
  - Color swatches are sibling `<button type="button" aria-pressed="...">` controls inside a `<div role="group">`, **never** nested inside the product `<a>` tag.
  - Care rows use native `<details>` and `<summary>` so keyboard `Enter`/`Space` works natively and focus never jumps.

### Checkpoint 3 — "How It Feels" / Competitor Benchmark Gravity Checkpoint
- **60fps Native Scroll & Landed GPU Cleanup**:
  - Normal document scrolling with zero scroll-jacking, zero sticky traps, and zero forced carousels.
  - Secondary compositions enter once at `15%` visibility (`translateY(12px → 0)` over `600ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`) and immediately set `data-landed="true"` on completion so `transform` and `will-change` are removed, keeping pure-vector serif and sans typography razor-sharp at every zoom level.
- **Tactile Restraint Worthy of Polène, Hereu, Loewe & Aesop**:
  - Fine-pointer hover enhancements (`240ms` bag view crossfade, `500ms` `scale(1.018)` story photo lift, `180ms` `4px` arrow travel, `160ms` `90°` `+` rotation) are gated behind `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` and disabled instantaneously under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
- **Iterative Per-Tout Evaluation & Fallback Principle (Living Guide, Not Blind Dogma)**:
  - While the `t=0` visual layout is strictly locked to **Authority #1 (`desktop-01-avela.png`)** and **Authority #2 (`mobile-01-avela.jpg`)**, the per-tout interaction suggestions in Section 4 serve as an informed starting guide rather than rigid dogma.
  - During implementation of each tout (`A00`–`A09`), we actively evaluate the interaction in the live rendered page on both desktop and mobile: if any drawer, rollover, or secondary control feels forced, over-engineered, or unnatural for that tout's hierarchy, we exercise in-the-moment design judgment to replace it with a more natural alternative or fall back cleanly to the baseline specification (`spec-desktop-01-avela.md`, `spec-mobile-01-avela.md`, and `react-interaction-contract.md`).
