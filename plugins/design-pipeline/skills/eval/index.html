eval is Stage 5 of jason-design-agent—the objective quality and originality gate governing the image-gen <-> polish loop across all 12 (4 x 3) 2k plates. Slots that pass all 6 gates freeze immediately at Round 1; slots that fail receive bounded [P0] / [P1] surgical directives routed to polish (up to min(design_agent_turns, 3) rounds maximum).

workflow checklist

  • step 1 — gate 01-anchor before batch expansion: inspect concepts/web/01-web-01-*.png via read_file + dimension check first. Block 02..12 generation until 01-anchor passes Gates 1–4 and Gate 6.
  • step 2 — inspect every 2k image individually (01..12): inspect each PNG at readable resolution (read_file) plus deterministic width/height/sRGB checks.
  • step 3 — cross-check against research/benchmarks.md + 5 screenshot folders: verify category-bar craft with zero copied benchmark fonts (avoid_fonts), brand hexes (avoid_palettes), logos, or layouts.
  • step 3.5 — tri-perspective brutal audit & anti-inching check: audit failing or candidate slots across the 3 perspectives (1. designer visual taste, 2. research benchmarks, 3. ruthless self-audit) and enforce the 10-dimension auto-correction matrix (python3 /usr/local/google/home/jfesta/design-kit/skills/design-critique/scripts/run_critique_swarm.py audit) so polish never inches one minor change at a time.
  • step 4 — write eval/concept-eval.md scorecard & route failing slots: mark every slot FROZEN or NEEDS_POLISH (round R of max 3) with 10-dimension auto-correction instructions.

1. the 6 concept evaluation gates

gatewhat eval verifiesfailure action
1. 01-anchor style & 2-font coherenceArt direction, 8-token hex family, corner radii, 1px hairlines, and strict <= 2 Google Font pairing (primary headline + secondary UI/body, or primary == secondary—zero scattered 3rd/4th fonts) match 01-anchor across all 12 plates.[P0] -> surgical polish referencing 01-anchor.
2. anti-clone originalityCross-checked against research/screenshots/01..05/, avoid_fonts, and avoid_palettes: 0 copied fonts, 0 copied palettes, 0 cloned layouts.[P0] -> immediate polish / token fix.
3. canon.md & coverage.md agreementBrand wordmark, domain vocabulary, entities, prices/metrics, active web nav (1/2/3), and app tab order (1/2/3) match canon.md.[P1] -> surgical polish on mismatched label/state.
4. >= 1K / 2K crystal-clear geometry, framing & footersEvery edge >= 1024px (min(w, h) >= 1024, canonical 2K lossless sRGB .png), crystal-clear acutance (sharpness_ratio >= 0.28, hf_detail_std >= 0.8 via lock_and_verify_plates.py verify), flat orthographic UI capture (0 bezels or angled desks), legible lettering, and complete footers on web plates.[P0] -> re-lock or re-frame via polish before Pre-Moodboard Checkpoint.
5. 4 x 3 distinctness (web · app · component · experience)Web covers 3 distinct pages; App covers 3 distinct screens of 1 app; Components (07..09) are 3 separate components; Experiences (10..12) are 3 unique interactive experiences.[P0] -> rewrite slot prompt within round cap.
6. Nano Banana 3-round budget guardrounds_used <= min(design_agent_turns, 3) (<= 3 hard ceiling); passing slots freeze at 1 round; zero 4th calls ever.Hard Stop -> log residual notes in eval/concept-eval.md for jason-dev-agent.
