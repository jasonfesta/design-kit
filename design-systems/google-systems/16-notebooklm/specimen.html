What evidence shows the beaver dam analogs reduced sediment load?

Three independent sources point the same direction. Downstream turbidity fell from 48 to 19 NTU after the analogs were installed in reach C 1, while sediment traps captured roughly 2.4 t of fine material over the summer 3.

The lecture notes that analogs work best where the channel gradient is below 2% — which matches reaches B and C but not the steeper reach A 2. Volunteers also reported new willow growth along the ponded margins 5.
