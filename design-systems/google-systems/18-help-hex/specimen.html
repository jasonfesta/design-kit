Turn on 2-Step Verification

With 2-Step Verification, you add an extra layer of security to your account in case your password is stolen.

ComputerAndroidiPhone & iPad
  1. Open your Google Account.
  2. In the navigation panel, select Security.
  3. Under “How you sign in to Google,” select 2-Step Verification › Get started.
  4. Follow the on-screen steps to add a passkey, phone or security key.
Tip: Passkeys let you sign in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
Important: If you use a work or school account, your administrator may manage this setting.
