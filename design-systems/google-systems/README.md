Complete, depot-verified installation of **10 High-Usage Documented Google Design Systems** extracted directly from `//depot/google3/` HEAD (`srcfs`) and Google's **Carbon Design System Token Compiler** (go/carbon-service-code-gen-mwtc), organized for the **WDA 37-Prompt Exercise (`#01`–`#37`)**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **1P Non-Purple Guarantee (`0.0% Purple` Across All 10 Google Systems)**
> Unlike public open-source Material 3 (which defaults to `#6750A4` purple), Google's internal 1P Carbon palettes anchor on **Google Blue (`#0B57D0` / `#1A73E8`)**, **Cloud & SecOps Ocean/Malachite (`#5DCEDE` / `#01D667`)**, **Geo Terra Aqua & Emerald (`#006876` / `#146C2E`)**, and **Ochre/Amber (`#A85D00` / `#F9AB00`)** — natively satisfying WDA's strict `<9.5%` purple guardrail out of the box.
