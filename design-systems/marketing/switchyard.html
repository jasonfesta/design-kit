Photography and frame ratios

Frames bare-metal rack photography and engineering studio portraits at fixed aspect ratios while network images decode or fail offline.

When to use

  • Pair 4 to 3 engineering portraits with matching hardware frames inside multi-column feature and proof rows.
  • Reserve wider 16 to 9 framing for full-span launch stages and horizontal conversion bands.
  • Render matching skeleton and surface fallback plates whenever slow links delay or block image requests.

How to apply it

  • Place right-facing portraits in the left column so the engineer gazes inward toward adjacent plates and copy.
  • Keep photographs at true brightness in both themes, adding an inset hairline so dark edges stay clear on obsidian backgrounds.
  • Set explicit width, height, and aspect-ratio properties so captions hold position before pixels arrive.
