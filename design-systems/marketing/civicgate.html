Documentary photography and captions

Shows real street signage, dashed bay lines, and neighbourhood outcomes where photography clarifies a rule faster than prose, while holding layout steady when images load late or stay off.

When to use

  • Include a street photograph when seeing real kerb lines or bay plates confirms the right council service.
  • Omit photography inside mid-form question steps and emergency service closures where residents need immediate plain text.

Usage and image rules

  • Choose real neighbourhood streets and pedestrians walking inward toward the page spine rather than posed portraits staring outward off the screen.
  • Declare exact image dimensions to stop layout shift, keep full true-color brightness in dark mode, and write factual captions below every frame.

Service start hero

Pairs a two-thirds public eligibility column and green start action with a one-third street context photograph and cobalt-ruled related guidance rail.

When to use

  • Open any resident permit, licence, or grant service where people check fees and documents before starting an application.
  • Switch to the urgent renewal or bay suspension states when an expiring plate or street works changes the main resident action.

Usage and image rules

  • Keep one high-contrast start button per screen and state completion time and logbook details underneath.
  • Keep the street photograph inside the side rail on standard start pages, and drop decorative photos during urgent renewal or suspension alerts.

Household tariff calculator

Lets residents calculate an exact 12-month street permit fee from two simple dropdowns for postcode zone and vehicle type without starting an account.

When to use

  • Provide before checkout whenever council charges vary across ward zones, emissions bands, and zero-fee statutory exemptions.
  • Limit the estimator to two plain dropdown inputs and tabular pound figures rather than sliders, steppers, or comparison charts.

Usage and image rules

  • Style select menus cleanly without raw browser chrome so touch targets stay generous and readable outdoors on phones.
  • Pull every fee from one shared schedule so calculator totals, state examples, and tariff tables agree to the pound.

Published tariff table and audit block

Displays statutory 6-month and 12-month emission bands alongside a measured neighbourhood outcome block rather than software tier cards or star ratings.

When to use

  • Publish whenever residents compare short-term and annual permit rates across every DVLA emission band in one ward zone.
  • Follow statutory tables with dated council audit findings so residents see the local effect of emission-banded charges.

Usage and image rules

  • Show savings in plain currency such as save £24, right-align fee columns in tabular numerals, and omit sort carets on short four-row schedules.
  • Keep aggregate audit percentages as unquoted factual statements paired with a real neighbourhood photograph or a compact text-only block.

Background-image reminder banner

Frames a two-field renewal reminder card or direct action prompt over an evening terrace background photograph while preserving clear lamp-lit street detail and high-contrast fallbacks.

When to use

  • Place at the foot of a public service start page for residents whose current permit is still valid and who want a single 28-day expiry email.
  • Switch to the solid high-contrast surface variant during civil contingencies or on dense statutory policy pages.

Usage and image rules

  • Anchor the background crop on the warm right-hand streetlamp and shade only behind the left form card so the lit terrace stays unobstructed.
  • Set an opaque dark fallback color behind every background image and strip background photography cleanly when forced-colors mode is active.

Registration input and alert states

Shows all four operational states of the UK number plate reminder control side by side so engineering and service teams implement validation, DVLA checks, and confirmation consistently.

When to use

  • Apply across every council service collecting a vehicle number plate, Blue Badge serial, or annual reminder email address.
  • Pair field-level error text with a top error summary whenever a submitted plate cannot be matched to UK format rules.

Usage and image rules

  • Validate on blur or submit rather than flagging incomplete plates while a resident is still typing the first letters.
  • Frame error summaries with an even two-pixel crimson border rather than thick left bars, and hold button width steady during DVLA checks.
