Zone B annual fees

All Zone B permits start from the standard £120 rate, with cleaner vehicles paying less each year.

Electric

Hybrid

Petrol or diesel

Resident bays operate Monday to Saturday, 8:30am to 6:30pm. Bank holidays are free for everyone.
